Original post:
 It is generally agreed that an internship in this field, and most other careers in general, is the best and easiest way to get experience that puts your foot in the door.

How hard is it to actually get an internship to begin with? Both for students who are currently enrolled in college, and for people who have already graduated.

Is it very competitive and are there enough positions? Does your GPA need to be really high? What’s the best way to get one?

This can apply to both paid and unpaid ones.
